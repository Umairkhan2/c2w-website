GOODS-TO-PERSON Q&A

Questions About Goods-to-Person Automation

Can we keep our existing inventory system?+

C2W can assess integration with your existing inventory or warehouse system. Compatibility depends on the available interfaces, data requirements and project scope.

What does C2W provide for the project?+

C2W provides consulting, system integration and implementation support. The agreed scope depends on your warehouse requirements and the systems involved.

How does Geek+ fit into the solution?+

Geek+ provides the robotic picking automation that moves goods toward operator workstations. C2W helps connect the inventory and workflow information around that operation.

What warehouse information is useful when discussing a project?+

Order volume, SKU characteristics, warehouse layout and existing systems are useful starting points for understanding the operation and possible integration needs.

What affects implementation timing and expected return?+

Timing and return vary with warehouse conditions, system requirements, equipment and project scope. These details should be reviewed during consultation rather than assumed from a general benchmark.
