MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES

Manufacturing, Simplified and Connected

Manage bills of materials, manufacturing orders, and inventory across locations, with connected visibility into components and finished goods.

Bill of Materials (BOM)

Define the components and quantities required for each finished product. Support decimal quantities and calculate BOM costs from component costs, keeping material requirements clear for manufacturing orders.

Manufacturing Order Management

Create manufacturing orders from sales orders and keep the sales order reference connected. Manage component quantities and complete multiple open manufacturing orders in a batch to reduce repetitive work.

Connected Inventory Updates

Keep component and finished-product quantities connected to manufacturing orders. When an order is completed, inventory reflects the materials consumed and the finished goods produced.

Multi-Location Management

View component and finished-product inventory across warehouses and storage locations. Track quantities by location and manage inventory transfers so your team knows where stock is available.

Material Replenishment

Use inventory quantities and minimum stock settings to identify materials that need replenishment. Keep purchasing connected to inventory so your team can review shortages and plan material purchases.

Batch and Lot Tracking

Record lot numbers and expiration dates for inventory items that require tracking. Use lot information and inventory movement history to help your team review stock and identify the lots involved in inventory transactions.

MANUFACTURING Q&A

Questions About C2W Manufacturing

Does C2W Manufacturing support Bills of Materials?+

Yes. Bills of Materials define the components and quantities required to produce finished products.

Can manufacturing orders use inventory data?+

Yes. Manufacturing Orders connect production activity with component availability, material consumption and finished inventory.

Where are BOM and manufacturing functions available?+

BOM and manufacturing functions are available in the Windows desktop app, with inventory data synchronized across the connected C2W platforms.

Does C2W support kits and bundles?+

Yes. Kits and bundles help organize products made from components that are sold or handled together.

Can manufacturing connect with purchasing and fulfillment?+

Yes. Receiving, inventory availability, production and finished-product fulfillment can remain connected through the broader inventory workflow.
