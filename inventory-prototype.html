INVENTORY ACTIVITY

Every Inventory Change Has a Reason

Follow the operational event behind a quantity change. C2W Inventory records inventory work in the context of the transaction that created it.

  1. RECEIVED

    Purchase order receiving adds incoming items to inventory.

  2. RESERVED

    Sales orders commit available stock before fulfillment.

  3. PICKED

    Picking confirms the items needed to fulfill an order.

  4. TRANSFERRED

    Transfers move stock between warehouses, locations or bins.

  5. ADJUSTED

    Counts and corrections update recorded quantities when inventory changes.

C2W INVENTORY Q&A

Questions About C2W Inventory

Practical answers about the inventory work your team handles every day.

What is C2W Inventory Management Software?+

C2W Inventory is inventory management software for managing stock, purchasing, sales orders, warehouse activity and related inventory work. It gives teams visibility into on hand, available, reserved and anticipated inventory while keeping activity tied to day-to-day operations.

Can I use C2W Inventory in a web browser?+

Yes. The Web App supports core inventory operations, including sales orders, purchase orders, receiving, picking, transfers and adjustments. BOM and manufacturing workflows are available in the Windows desktop app, which also supports advanced tasks such as batch imports, batch printing and deeper administration. Android SmartScan supports mobile scanning workflows.

Can C2W Inventory track stock across multiple locations?+

Yes. C2W Inventory can manage inventory across multiple warehouses and locations, helping teams see where stock is stored and move inventory between locations when needed.

How does C2W Inventory show available inventory?+

C2W distinguishes between on hand, available, reserved and anticipated quantities. This helps users understand what is physically recorded, what is available after reservations, and what inventory is expected to arrive.

Can I set reorder points and monitor low stock?+

Yes. Reorder points and reorder quantities can be maintained at the item level. Teams can use these values with current and anticipated inventory to identify products that may need replenishment.

Does C2W Inventory support lot numbers, serial numbers and expiration dates?+

Yes. C2W Inventory supports lot tracking, serial tracking and expiration-date tracking for products that require additional traceability. FEFO workflows are available for expiration-sensitive inventory where applicable.

Can warehouse teams use barcode scanners or mobile devices?+

Yes. C2W supports barcode-based inventory workflows and Android SmartScan for receiving, picking, transfers, adjustments and inventory-related scanning.

Does C2W Inventory support manufacturing?+

Yes. C2W Manufacturing provides BOM and manufacturing workflows in the Windows desktop app, alongside connected inventory for components, assemblies, kits and finished goods.
